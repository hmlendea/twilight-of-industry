## Capability And Mechanism Checks

- Check each material capability, including baseline energy sources, against established local IRF limits and deployment evidence. Full-strength IRF does not support dependable industrial nuclear power; an experimental or minor reactor is not automatically an exemption or a dominant energy source. Establish a canon-compatible mechanism and restricted contribution, or retain supported sources.
- Specify communication medium, relay spacing or topology where material, localisation reference, latency, power, and failure limits. Shared frequencies, encryption, or GPS-equivalent coordinates do not explain transmission via attenuating ice or water; use justified local acoustic, wired, or other established infrastructure.
- Distinguish unmanned cargo vehicles, passenger conveyances, medical personnel, and robotic equipment. For vehicle losses, state personnel presence, casualty or survival status where established, and how relief continues; preserve indeterminate status explicitly.
- Explain environmental causation at the affected route's location and depth. Do not import terrestrial seasons onto other worlds without an orbital or local operational explanation; surface temperature alone does not establish subsurface route closure.
- Explain preservation conditions for ancient vulnerable materials, including water exclusion, environmental control, custody, or surviving copies, rather than presuming exposed manuscripts endure inundation.

## Numerical And Enforcement Checks

- Define physical quantities, units, geometry, reference conditions, exposure period, and model uncertainty where consequential. An intrusion distance must identify the interface and affected aquifer; a percentage deterioration estimate requires a reference rate and material conditions. Qualify unsupported precision.
- Recalculate recurrence intervals from dated anchors; verify duration and amplitude separately. If a stated interval conflicts with established dates, expose the conflict and consult founding passages instead of propagating either value without verification.
- Test each enforcement case against the policy as written: initial and projected value in each relevant category, threshold crossing, responsible actor, affected actor, required action, and deadline. Distinguish relative percentage change from percentage-point change or an absolute adequacy threshold.
- Distinguish preventive orders, emergency activation, immediate safeguards, and extended remediation. Explain authorised exceptions explicitly; a longer remediation interval does not silently replace an emergency deadline.
- Verify receiving habitat, food, water, thermal, transport, and emergency capacity prior to relocation, especially when the destination is endangered. Surplus in one reserve category does not compensate for another category's deficit.
- Environmental compensation or credits require non-tradable local safety limits for water, ecology, and irreplaceable heritage, plus physically comparable impacts and enforceable verification; distant restoration does not cancel local damage.
- For external audits, distinguish local inspection from remote review. Establish personnel location, feasible travel or communication, inspection rights, local expertise, evidence provenance, authentication, and verification limits. Neutrality alone supplies none of these capabilities.
