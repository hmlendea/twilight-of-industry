## ROADMAP Continuity Rules

- When creating or revising a chapter, you must read `ROADMAP.md` before drafting any chapter content.
- Identify pending items in `ROADMAP.md` that are due in, overlap with, or are naturally advanced by the chapter period.
- Include every relevant pending item in the new chapter with concrete dated progress, outcomes, or explicit continuation state.
- If a pending item remains unresolved after this chapter, record meaningful progress in the chapter and keep the unresolved remainder consistent with `ROADMAP.md`.
- If a pending roadmap item is fully implemented and fully finalised in the chapter timeline, remove that item from `ROADMAP.md`.
- If a pending roadmap item is only partially implemented, update its `ROADMAP.md` entry to reflect what remains pending.
- When new chapter content introduces open-ended developments, deferred consequences, or multi-period arcs that should continue later, add or update detailed entries in `ROADMAP.md` describing the unresolved thread, expected continuation direction, and key future checkpoints.
- Add or update a `ROADMAP.md` continuity record whenever chapter content introduces or changes any of the following, even when it would not otherwise qualify as an open-ended arc:
	- Every launch that has departed but has not reached its intended destination by the end of the chapter, including its origin, destination, departure date, expected arrival date or window, and latest known status.
	- Every established contact and every lost contact, including the participants, date or period, communication method where known, and current contact status.
	- Every adopted policy, including the adopting faction, adoption date or period, scope, implementation status, and unresolved obligations or consequences.
	- Every faction appearance, disappearance, or material status change, including the date or period and latest known political, territorial, demographic, or institutional status.
	- Every material relationship change between factions, including the participants, date or period, prior relationship, current relationship, and unresolved consequences.
- Retain each mandatory continuity record until a later chapter explicitly resolves, supersedes, or terminates it; do not infer resolution from elapsed time or silence.
- Do not finalise a new or revised chapter until the `ROADMAP.md` cross-check has been completed.
