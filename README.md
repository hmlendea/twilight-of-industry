# Twilight of Industry

Twilight of Industry is an AI-assisted worldbuilding repository that chronicles a long-form speculative timeline, from a non-industrial 2026 baseline to deep-future civilisational transformations across Terra, Luna, Mars, Titan, and extra-solar branches.

## ✨ Features

- Chronological chapter corpus in [chapters/](./chapters/), covering multiple eras and narrative arcs.
- Structured chapter frontmatter and event-oriented composition conventions.
- Forward-planning document in [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md) to track pending arcs and continuity obligations.

## ⚠️ Known Limitations

- The timeline remains incomplete; several long-horizon arcs are intentionally unresolved.
- Narrative density and chapter length are non-uniform across periods.
- Some periods are intentionally represented as interval chapters rather than single-year entries.
