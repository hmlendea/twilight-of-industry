# 11000 — Year 11,000

Almost one thousand years after the impact.

Relative to the preceding period (10,300).

At this stage,

The rate of change has already slowed dramatically.

Not because innovation has stopped.

Because humanity has finally entered the technological era it had intellectually prepared for over the previous 10,000 years.

---

## The Great Compression

Historians often illustrate the first millennium after the impact like this:

| Years | Equivalent in our history |
| --- | --- |
| 10,029–10,080 | Steam → Electricity |
| 10,080–10,140 | Electronics |
| 10,140–10,220 | Digital revolution |
| 10,220–10,350 | Early Space Age |
| 10,350–10,600 | Interplanetary civilisation |
| 10,600–11,000 | Mature technological civilisation |

Humanity effectively experiences ten centuries of our technological history...

...in under one thousand years.

---

# Earth

Earth no longer resembles an industrial planet.

It resembles a garden.

Factories still exist.

Mining still exists.

But almost every heavy industry has moved:
- underground
- offshore
- into orbit
- onto the Moon

Not because Earth is exhausted.

Because preserving Earth has become almost sacred.

---

# Cities

The oldest cities are now over **10,000 years old**.

Yet they remain inhabited.

Consider walking through a city where:
- the oldest district dates from year 400
- another from year 2800
- another from year 7600
- another from year 10,600

Every era survives.

The city is effectively a museum of civilisation.

---

# The Moon

The Moon has become humanity's industrial centre.

Why?

No atmosphere.

Low gravity.

Plentiful minerals.

The first gigantic factories are built there instead of Earth.

The irony is beautiful.

Earth skipped the dirty Industrial Revolution almost entirely.

The Moon had it instead.

---

# Mars

Mars contains millions of people.

Not because Earth became overcrowded.

Because exploration became humanity's defining cultural value.

Settlements spread across:
- Valles Marineris
- Hellas Basin
- the poles

Many are underground.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

Now something genuinely different happens.

Remember:

This civilisation possessed:
- 10,000 years of philosophy
- 10,000 years of ethics
- 10,000 years of legal history

before creating advanced AI.

As a result...

AI develops under extraordinarily mature ethical frameworks.

Rather than asking:

> "Can we build it?"

The first question is:

> "Should we?"

Entire centuries of debate precede genuinely autonomous artificial minds.

---

# Education

Education changes fundamentally.

Instead of memorising facts...

Everyone has instant access to:

The Complete Human Archive.

Every surviving document.

Every scientific paper.

Every law.

Every work of literature.

Every archaeological record.

Knowledge becomes a public utility.

---

# Biology

Medicine advances enormously.

Life expectancy at birth:

Perhaps

105–125 years.

Adults with continuous access to advanced treatment commonly live **120–150 years**.

Not through magic.

Simply through:
- organ replacement
- regenerative medicine
- cancer elimination
- genetic repair

---

# Space

Humanity reaches the outer Solar System.

Permanent settlements exist on:
- Europa
- Titan
- Ganymede
- Callisto

Asteroid mining becomes routine.

---

# The Asteroid

The impact crater itself becomes...

The holiest place on Earth.

Not in a religious sense.

In a civilisational sense.

People travel there throughout their lives.

A monument simply reads:

> **"Everything changed here."**

No further explanation is considered necessary.
